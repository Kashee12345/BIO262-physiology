BIO 262 Study Guides

Fourteen lessons, one for each module. Read a page, play with the model, then answer the five questions at the end.

How to use these
  • Each page covers one module. Give it twenty to thirty minutes.
  • Every page has a working model you can drag, step through, or break. Use it. That is the part that sticks.
  • Every page ends with five questions. Every answer choice is explained, right or wrong, so read them.
  • Download PDF at the top of any page prints a clean copy, with an answer key at the end. Or take the ready-made file from the PDF link on any card.

A note on module numbers. These pages are numbered to match the Canvas shell, which is what you see week to week. The exam blueprint, the practice questions and the exam review guides on this site number Modules 4 to 7 differently. There, Module 4 is Bones and Skeletal, 5 is Muscle and Nervous Tissue, 6 is Brain and ANS, and 7 is Special Senses. Modules 1 to 3 and 8 to 14 are the same in both. If they disagree, study by topic, not by number.
