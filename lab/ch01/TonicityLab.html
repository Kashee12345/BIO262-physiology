Osmolality and Tonicity Lab

Osmolarity counts every dissolved particle. Tonicity counts only the effective osmoles — the particles that cannot cross the membrane and therefore actually pull water. Build a patient's plasma from sodium, glucose and urea, then put a red cell in a bath and watch what the effective particles do to its volume.
