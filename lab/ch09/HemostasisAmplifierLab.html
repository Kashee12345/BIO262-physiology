Hemostasis: An Amplifier With Brakes

Intact endothelium is actively anti-thrombotic, so clotting begins as the removal of an inhibition plus the exposure of a trigger. What follows is genuine positive feedback — activated platelets release ADP and thromboxane A₂ that activate more platelets; thrombin activates factors V, VIII and XI and makes more thrombin. Cut the vessel below, then take away one component at a time and watch the burst fail, or take away the brakes and watch it refuse to stop.
