Four Pressures and One Reflex

Where fluid sits is decided by four numbers at the capillary wall: NFP = (Pc − Pif) − (πc − πif). How hard the blood is pushed is defended in seconds by the baroreflex. Move a Starling force and watch the capillary switch from filtering to reabsorbing along its length — then stand a patient up, blunt the reflex, and watch the pressure fail to come back.
