The Erythropoietin Loop

The controlled variable is not red cell count and not hematocrit — it is oxygen delivery to the renal cortex. Break one link below (the oxygen supply, the kidney that senses it, or the marrow that answers) and run the clock forward ninety days. Then do the thing that localizes every one of these disorders: read hemoglobin and erythropoietin together.
Pick a disturbance

The loop, drawn

The sensor and the integrator are the same cell. Peritubular fibroblasts make HIF-2α continuously; oxygen-requiring prolyl hydroxylases destroy it within minutes when oxygen is plentiful. Oxygen availability is read directly as the rate of an enzyme reaction.
