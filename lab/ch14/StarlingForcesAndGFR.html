Starling Forces and the 180 Liters

Filtration is not a search for waste. It is a physical event driven by a net Starling pressure of only about 10 mmHg across a barrier so permeable that 10 mmHg yields 125 mL/min. Move the four pressures, the filtration coefficient and the two arterioles below, and watch NFP, GFR, renal blood flow and filtration fraction respond. Then switch autoregulation off and see how far GFR drifts as mean arterial pressure swings.

PGC is not a slider. It is the pressure between two resistance vessels, so it is computed from mean arterial pressure and the two arteriolar tones. Constrict upstream and it falls; constrict downstream and it rises. That is the whole reason the glomerulus behaves differently from every other capillary bed.
