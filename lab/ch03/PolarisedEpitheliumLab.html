The Polarized Epithelium

An epithelial cell is a machine, and its asymmetry is the motor: the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase sits basolateral, builds a sodium battery, and every apical transporter spends it. Poison the pump, move it to the apical membrane, or open the tight junction, and watch intracellular sodium, glucose uptake, water flow and the direction of transport all follow. Nothing here pumps water — find the solute first.
