Skin as a Control Organ

Core temperature is held near 37 °C by a negative feedback loop whose two main effectors are both in the skin: cutaneous blood flow, variable more than a hundredfold, and hypotonic eccrine sweat. Drive the loop with heat, humidity and exercise, then disable an effector and watch it fail. The sweat duct and the repair timeline are on the other two benches.
Scenarios
Break an effector
The coil secretes; the duct edits
The clear cells of the coil secrete an essentially isotonic, plasma-like precursor near 140 mmol/L sodium, driven by NKCC1 with chloride following and water dragged osmotically. The duct then reabsorbs sodium through ENaC and chloride through CFTR — but the duct is relatively water-impermeable, so salt leaves and water stays.
