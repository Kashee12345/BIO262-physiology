The Skin Barrier and Its Repair Loop

The barrier is not the cells — it is the lipid mortar between dead corneocytes, and it is defended by a genuine negative feedback loop with a calcium gradient as its sensor. Strip it, watch transepidermal water loss jump more than tenfold, then let the clock run and watch lamellar bodies discharge and lipid synthesis rebuild it. Then break one component of the loop and see what a barrier without feedback looks like.
