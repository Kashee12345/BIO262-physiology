Nine Liters a Day — and the One Reflex the Cortex Can Veto

Two liters are drunk and about seven are secreted, so nine liters arrive at the small intestine every day and only about 150 mL leaves in stool. The colon’s spare capacity is only four to five liters, and that narrow margin is the whole story of diarrhea. Push the ledger below, use fasting as a diagnostic test, then take control of the two anal sphincters and see which one the cortex can actually command.
