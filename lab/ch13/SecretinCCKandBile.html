Secretin, CCK and the Enterohepatic Circulation

Chyme leaves the stomach at about pH 2; pancreatic enzymes work best near pH 7 to 8. Two feedback loops fix that mismatch — secretin brings the water and the buffer, CCK brings the enzymes and a bolus of concentrated bile. Deliver acid and fat below, then disable CFTR, the duct, the acinar cells or the terminal ileum, and watch which failure makes fat absorption collapse and why.

2 · Break a limb of the machinery

Pancreatic proteases leave the gland as inactive zymogens. Duodenal brush-border enteropeptidase cleaves trypsinogen to trypsin, and trypsin then activates all the rest — including more trypsinogen. That autocatalytic step is a genuine, tightly walled-off use of positive feedback, and its purpose is speed once the threshold is crossed.
