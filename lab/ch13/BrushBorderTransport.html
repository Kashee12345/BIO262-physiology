The Brush Border: Sodium Gradients, Surface Area and Oral Rehydration

Nearly every uptake step in the small intestine is paid for by one pump: the basolateral Na+/K+-ATPase, which holds intracellular sodium near 12 mM against 140 mM outside. Set what is in the lumen, poison the pump, flatten the villi — and watch SGLT1 flux, the water that follows it, and the daily stool volume all move together. Water never leads.
