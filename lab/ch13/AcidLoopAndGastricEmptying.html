The Parietal Cell, the Somatostatin Brake and Metered Emptying

The parietal cell holds a hydrogen ion concentration roughly three million times higher in the lumen than in blood, and a single negative feedback loop decides when it stops: antral pH is the variable, the D cell is the sensor, somatostatin is the signal, the parietal cell is the effector. Serve a meal below, then disable one limb of that loop at a time — and watch what happens to pH, to acid output and, revealingly, to gastrin.
